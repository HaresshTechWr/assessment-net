# Security & Compliance

Netomi maintains the following certifications and compliance standards for enterprise clients.

## Certifications

| Standard | Status |
|---|---|
| SOC 2 Type II | Certified — annual audit |
| HIPAA | Available for qualifying enterprise plans |
| GDPR | Data processing agreements available |
| ISO 27001 | Certified |
| CCPA | Compliant |
| PCI-DSS | Available for financial services clients |

## Data Privacy

All customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Netomi does not sell or share customer data with third parties.

## Enterprise Compliance Requests

For compliance documentation, security questionnaires, or data processing agreements — contact your Netomi account team or email security@netomi.com.
